Early life and education

Lay phyu was born on May 19,1965,in Inle Lake,Shan state Myanmar,and graduated from Mandalay University with a major in English. Lay Phyu started his music journey by covering English rock songs in Burmese before eventually rising to massive fame with his debut album,Gandarya Lamin(desert moon)
